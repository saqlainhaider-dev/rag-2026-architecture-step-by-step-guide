# Acme Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-15
Owner: Finance / Support
Access: public

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of purchase for software subscriptions, and within **14 days** for hardware accessories.

Refunds are only available if:
- The order was placed with a verified Acme account
- The product has not been marked as consumed beyond the trial period
- Hardware is returned in original packaging with all accessories

## How to Request a Refund

1. Open the Acme Support portal
2. Choose **Billing → Request refund**
3. Provide the order ID and a short reason
4. Support responds within **2 business days**

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–10 business days**.

## Non-refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund:
- Custom enterprise onboarding packages after kickoff has started
- Third-party marketplace add-ons sold by partners
- Gift cards and promotional credits

## Partial Refunds

If only one seat in a multi-seat subscription is unused, Acme may issue a **pro-rated partial refund** for that seat. Partial refunds require manager approval on the support ticket.
